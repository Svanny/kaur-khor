# Mobile Responsiveness

## Audit Target

Improve mobile and narrow viewport usability where the product feels cramped, clipped, or slow.

## Inspect

- Responsive navigation, embedded browser layouts, rotated phone views, forms, chart/table fallbacks, touch targets, and scroll behavior.

## Real Opportunity Criteria

The mobile surface hides content, blocks interaction, overflows, uses wrong layout mode, or feels unstable during resize/scroll.

## Fix Constraints

Verify at concrete mobile viewports and preserve desktop-primary behavior.

## Verification Required

Use mobile viewport screenshots or responsive tests. Record viewport and evidence in the item notes.
