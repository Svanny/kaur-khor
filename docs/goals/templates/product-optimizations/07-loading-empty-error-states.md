# Loading, Empty, and Error States

## Audit Target

Improve perceived performance and clarity during loading, empty, and failure states.

## Inspect

- Skeletons, placeholders, disabled states, save feedback, error copy, retry affordances, first-run empty pages, and partial failure UI.

## Real Opportunity Criteria

The app feels frozen, broken, misleading, blank, or unrecoverable during a normal wait, empty state, or failure.

## Fix Constraints

Do not hide real failures or invent success states. Keep copy concise and user-facing.

## Verification Required

Use focused tests, UI checks, or screenshots for each state. Record evidence in the item notes.
