# Screenshots and Captures

## Audit Target

Test screenshot, capture, and visual regression evidence flows.

## Inspect

- Capture routes, screenshot helpers, visual matrix outputs, key product pages, chart-heavy pages, and generated-state captures.

## Real Finding Criteria

Captures are blank, misframed, unstable, missing expected state, or fail to represent the tested UI.

## Fix Constraints

Do not treat a route-load assertion as visual verification when screenshots are required.

## Verification Required

Generate or inspect the relevant capture evidence. Record artifact paths or screenshots in the item notes.
