# Known Fragile UI Areas

## Audit Target

Test UI areas known to be dynamic, dense, or regression-prone.

## Inspect

- Charts, drawers, configurable tables, inventory health views, logs, tickets, dynamic detail pages, right rails, nested panels, and capture surfaces.

## Real Finding Criteria

The area collapses, clips, disagrees with data, loses scroll access, breaks density, or becomes unusable under realistic populated or dependent state.

## Fix Constraints

Verify at full route or Electron geometry when component-level checks are insufficient.

## Verification Required

Record target area, data state, viewport/window size, interaction path, and screenshot or test evidence in the item notes.
